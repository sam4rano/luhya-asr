1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
───────────────────
Fine-tuning Facebook's Wav2Vec2-BERT 2.0 (580M parameters) for Automatic Speech
Recognition in Luhya, a Bantu language spoken in Kenya. This is a low-resource
setting: ~20 hours of labelled speech.

Model published on HuggingFace Hub: Sam4rano/luhya-asr-w2v-BERT


2. WHY THIS SETUP?
───────────────────

  Model: Wav2Vec2-BERT 2.0 (facebook/w2v-bert-2.0)
  ├─ Built on the Wav2Vec2 self-supervised learning framework, pretrained on
  │  1 million+ hours of multilingual speech. Proven to outperform XLS-R, MMS,
  │  and Whisper variants for low-resource ASR fine-tuning.
  ├─ 580M parameters: 24 Conformer layers, 1024 hidden size, 16 attention heads
  ├─ Uses SeamlessM4T feature extractor (80-dim mel spectrograms at 16 kHz)
  └─ Uses CTC (Connectionist Temporal Classification) for alignment-free training,
     ideal for low-resource languages where forced-alignment data is not available.

  Training setup choices:
  ├─ Batch size 4 × gradient accumulation 8 → effective batch 32
  ├─ Learning rate 5e-5 with 10% warmup (follows official HF blog recommendation)
  ├─ bf16 mixed precision → better numerical stability than fp16 on supported GPUs
  ├─ Gradient checkpointing ON → saves VRAM, essential for 580M model on T4 (16GB)
  ├─ Frozen feature encoder → preserves pretrained acoustic representations
  ├─ Final adapter layer (TDNN, kernel=3, stride=2) → lightweight task-specific
  │  adaptation without full fine-tuning of the entire encoder
  └─ Early stop at 18,400 steps (~2 epochs) → model converged quickly on small data

  Data preprocessing:
  ├─ 16 kHz mono audio
  ├─ NFC Unicode normalization + accent mapping
  ├─ Lowercasing, whitespace normalisation
  ├─ Character set: a-z, digits 0-9, basic punctuation (!'*,-.:;?)
  └─ 50-token CTC vocabulary including word delimiter |, [UNK], [PAD]

4. TRAINING TIME
────────────────
Total steps: 18,400 (early stop at ~2 epochs out of 25 planned)
Estimated wall-clock time: 3 – 6 hours on a T4 GPU
The model converged quickly because:
  ● Small dataset (~20h) means fast epoch iteration
  ● Pretrained Wav2Vec2-BERT features are already highly transferable
  ● CTC loss is efficient (no autoregressive decoding during training)
  ● bf16 + gradient checkpointing maximised GPU throughput


5. FINAL RESULTS
────────────────
| Metric              | Validation   |
|---------------------|--------------|
| Word Error Rate     | 43.45%       |
| Character Error Rate| 11.36%       |
| Eval Loss (CTC)     | 0.4551       |
| Composite Score *   | 72.60 / 100  |

* Score = (1 - 0.5×WER - 0.5×CER) × 100

Interpretation:
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  For a low-resource language with only 20 hours of training
  data, a WER of 43.45% is a strong baseline. The much lower
  CER (11.36%) indicates the model learns character-level
  patterns well; word-level errors are largely driven by the
  small vocabulary coverage.

  Typical first-pass WER for low-resource ASR fine-tuning
  ranges from 35–60%. This result is in the competitive range
  and provides a solid foundation for iterative improvement.
